    Setup guide

    Skipped something during setup? Everything is here. Each step takes about a minute.

    Connect your AI

    Add Thread as a connector, and your AI can read and save to your memory.

    Add to Claude
    1. Claude: press Add to Claude. It opens claude.ai with Thread filled in: press Add, then Connect. On a phone, do it in your browser (the Claude app can't add connectors); Thread then works in the app too.
    2. ChatGPT (Plus or higher): Settings → turn on Developer mode → add a new app with this address (Authentication: OAuth). Then in each chat, tap + → Developer mode → pick Thread.
    3. When it asks, press Continue as you (or sign in with your Thread email and password).

    On this laptop, Claude Code already uses Thread. Once Thread is online, the address to add in the Claude and ChatGPT apps will show up here.

    Canvas

    What each connection can do: Canvas is read-only (deadlines, and with an access token also syllabi, announcements, pages and assignment details; Thread never submits anything). Google Calendar reads your events and adds ones you ask for. Your Canvas token, feed link and Google sign-in are stored encrypted on Thread's server.

    1. Open Canvas Calendar.
    2. Press Calendar Feed (bottom right on a computer; on a phone, scroll to the bottom) and copy the link.
    3. Paste it here.

    Thread only reads your deadlines. The calendar feed doesn't say what you've already submitted; for that and syllabus files, use .

    1. In Canvas, open Account → Settings.
    2. Under Approved Integrations, press + New Access Token. Name it "Thread" and leave the date blank.
    3. Copy the token and paste it here.

    Thread only reads your classes and due dates. It never submits or changes anything in Canvas.

      Report a bug or idea

      Goes straight to the person building Thread. Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened. You can also just tell your AI ("report a Thread bug: …") and it will send it for you.

      We'll include your screen size and browser so we can reproduce it, never your notes.

      Deleted a chat by accident? It's still here. Thread keeps a copy of your ChatGPT and Claude chats for 10 days after it first saves them, then deletes it for good. Your AI can search them too.

      Turn on chat backup

      A small Chrome extension copies each chat as you use chatgpt.com or claude.ai on a computer. It can't see the phone apps, and it never sees your passwords.

      1. Install the Thread Backup extension in Chrome.
      2. Press Make my backup key and copy it.
      3. Open the extension, paste your Thread address and the key, and press Save.

      Copy it now. For safety, Thread only shows it once. Making a new key turns the old one off.
